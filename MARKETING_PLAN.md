# Phase 5: Organic Marketing Plan for Thumora AI

Prepared on April 20, 2026.

This plan is for zero-dollar acquisition only. No ads, no paid placements, no paid creators, no paid tooling beyond what the product already uses.

## Goal

Get Thumora AI from "working product" to "repeatable organic growth" by focusing on one narrow market first: YouTube creators who care about packaging and need faster thumbnail iteration.

Primary 90-day outcome:

- Reach the first 100 activated users.
- Reach the first 10 paying creators.
- Learn which organic channel produces the best activated users, not just the most clicks.

## What Thumora Can Honestly Sell Today

Based on the current codebase and public app surface, the strongest marketable features are:

- AI thumbnail editing from a blank canvas or uploaded image
- Reference-image and sketch-based workflows
- Idea Assistant for thumbnail directions
- YouTube thumbnail import
- Creator tools such as Remove Background, Polish / Enhance, Upscale 4K, and Insert Me
- Free Hobby plan with 3 credits every 30 days
- Paid recurring plans through Whop

This is enough to market as a focused thumbnail workflow product.

## What Thumora Should Not Claim Yet

- Do not market it as a general-purpose AI image app.
- Do not market it as a full team operating system for media companies.
- Do not promise "viral thumbnails" or guaranteed CTR lifts.
- Do not lead with weak or unfinished surfaces if they are not part of the strongest user journey.

The cleanest positioning is narrower and stronger.

## Best-Fit Customer

Start with one main ICP instead of trying to win "all creators."

Primary ICP:

- English-speaking YouTube creators with roughly 1,000 to 100,000 subscribers
- They publish at least weekly
- They already believe thumbnails matter
- They do not want Photoshop friction
- They are willing to test multiple thumbnail directions

Best early sub-niches:

- Commentary
- Education
- Business
- Tech
- Reaction
- Faceless channels that still need strong packaging

Later markets:

- Agencies
- Large media teams
- Broad AI design users

## Positioning

Recommended positioning:

> Thumora AI is the thumbnail studio for YouTube creators who need faster iteration, cleaner packaging, and creator-specific workflows instead of generic AI image generation.

Short tagline options:

- Create better thumbnails faster.
- Thumbnail workflows, not generic prompts.
- From rough idea to publish-ready thumbnail.

## Organic Growth Model

This product should grow through proof, not slogans.

The zero-budget loop should be:

1. Publish useful thumbnail content.
2. Show real before/after transformations.
3. Turn that proof into creator conversations.
4. Turn creator conversations into case studies and testimonials.
5. Reuse those case studies across social, Product Hunt, and search pages.

## The 5 Priority Channels

### 1. Short-form social content

Use the channels already present in the footer:

- X
- TikTok
- Instagram
- LinkedIn

Priority use:

- X for maker/build-in-public and creator teardowns
- TikTok and Instagram for visual before/after proof
- LinkedIn for founder story, product progress, and creator-business positioning

Post formats:

- Before/after thumbnail makeovers
- "3 ways I would improve this thumbnail"
- Sketch to final thumbnail clips
- Prompt breakdowns
- Tool-specific demos: Remove Background, Polish, 4K, YouTube import
- Weekly founder post: what creators struggled with this week

Rule:

- Every post should teach something or show proof.
- Do not post generic "AI is changing everything" content.

### 2. YouTube itself

Thumora sells to YouTubers, so YouTube must become a first-class marketing channel.

Content types:

- 3 to 8 minute thumbnail teardown videos
- Shorts from those teardowns
- Posts with before/after comparisons, polls, and mini lessons

Use cases:

- Roast weak thumbnails and explain why they miss
- Rebuild a creator thumbnail in Thumora
- Compare 3 thumbnail directions for one video topic
- Show how to move from idea to image using the Idea Assistant and Studio

Dogfood move:

- Use your own YouTube videos to test thumbnails with YouTube's native A/B testing
- Share the results publicly

### 3. Manual creator outreach

This should be high-touch and targeted, not spam.

Weekly process:

- Pick 20 to 30 creators in one niche
- Review their last 10 thumbnails
- Make 1 quick teardown or remake
- Send a short DM or email with the image and reasoning
- Ask for permission to share the result publicly if they like it

Offer:

- "I made 2 stronger thumbnail directions for your next upload using Thumora. Want the files?"

The point is not scale on day one. The point is proof and user language.

### 4. Product Hunt

Product Hunt should be treated as a launch amplifier, not the entire strategy.

Launch only when:

- The product is live
- The signup flow works
- The core Studio flow works
- You have at least a few real examples, screenshots, and a short demo

Do not launch a waitlist-only version.

### 5. Search and case-study pages

Do not start with a generic blog.

The current repo intentionally does not center blog content, so the first search strategy should be:

- public case studies
- comparison pages
- use-case pages
- original thumbnail education pages with real examples

Good page ideas:

- `AI thumbnail editor for YouTube creators`
- `Thumbnail makeover examples`
- `How to test YouTube thumbnails`
- `Thumbnail ideas for commentary videos`
- `Thumbnail ideas for education channels`
- `Photoshop vs AI thumbnail workflow`

Each page should include:

- original before/after images
- specific creator problems
- exact workflow
- CTA to the free plan

## Content Pillars

Use five recurring pillars so content does not become random.

### Pillar A: Makeovers

- Before/after transformations
- "Fix this thumbnail" posts
- Weak thumbnail to strong thumbnail breakdowns

### Pillar B: Education

- Why thumbnails fail
- How to create visual hierarchy
- When to use faces, contrast, text, and negative space

### Pillar C: Process

- Idea Assistant to final image
- YouTube import to polished version
- Reference image to final thumbnail

### Pillar D: Proof

- Real creator examples
- A/B testing results
- User testimonials
- "This creator picked version 2"

### Pillar E: Build in public

- What changed this week
- What users asked for
- What prompt patterns work best
- What not to do with AI thumbnails

## Weekly Publishing Cadence

This is aggressive but realistic for zero-dollar growth.

- 3 short-form makeover videos
- 2 short educational posts
- 1 founder/build-in-public thread or post
- 1 longer YouTube teardown video
- 5 to 10 manual creator outreach messages per weekday
- 1 weekly recap post with lessons and best results

One piece of work should feed multiple channels. For example:

- Record one thumbnail teardown
- Cut it into 3 short clips
- Turn the main lesson into an X thread
- Turn the screenshots into an Instagram carousel
- Turn the lesson into a search page or case study later

## First 30 Days

### Week 1: Tighten the message and proof

- Lock the core positioning around "thumbnail studio for YouTube creators"
- Create 10 strong before/after assets from real or internal examples
- Record 3 demo clips showing Studio, Idea Assistant, and YouTube import
- Define one CTA for all posts: free plan signup
- Create a simple tracking sheet with source, signup, activation, and payment columns

### Week 2: Start daily proof distribution

- Publish daily short-form before/after content
- Start the first 50 creator outreach messages
- Collect objections and copy language from replies
- Turn the best 3 examples into public case-study drafts

### Week 3: Double down on what gets saves, replies, and signups

- Keep only the top 2 content formats
- Publish the first long-form YouTube teardown
- Ask early users for a one-line testimonial
- Prepare Product Hunt assets if activation quality looks good

### Week 4: Launch and compound

- Ship Product Hunt only if the product is genuinely ready
- Publish a "how we built this for creators" founder post
- Publish 2 case-study pages or equivalent public proof pages
- Review which channel produced activated users, not vanity traffic

## Messaging Angles That Fit Thumora

Use these consistently:

- Faster than opening Photoshop for every thumbnail idea
- Better for creator workflows than generic AI art apps
- Useful even before generation because the Idea Assistant helps shape the concept
- Start free, test ideas, upgrade only when the workflow becomes recurring
- Bring your own image, sketch, or YouTube reference instead of starting from nothing

Avoid these:

- "Make viral thumbnails in one click"
- "Replace your designer forever"
- "Unlimited AI magic"
- "Best AI image generator"

## Product-Led Growth Hooks

Use what the product already gives you.

- Free Hobby credits are the trial
- Before/after exports are shareable proof
- YouTube import creates a natural "improve your last thumbnail" hook
- Idea Assistant creates content-worthy outputs even before image generation

The best CTA is not "buy now." It is:

- Try your next thumbnail in Thumora
- Test 3 directions for free
- Bring in your last YouTube thumbnail and improve it

## Launch Readiness Gaps To Fix Before Pushing Traffic Hard

These are not blockers for writing content, but they matter before a larger launch:

- The `/contact` page currently simulates form success instead of sending to a real support system
- The Discord link on `/contact` is a placeholder and should either be real or removed
- Public proof is still thin; add more visible real examples before a wider launch push
- Attribution should capture acquisition source, not only auth method

## Metrics

Use the current stack before adding any paid analytics tooling.

Free stack:

- Vercel Analytics for traffic trends
- Supabase for user and generation data
- A spreadsheet or Notion database for weekly review

Core metrics:

- Visitor to signup rate
- Signup to first generation within 24 hours
- Activated user to second generation within 7 days
- Activated user to paid plan conversion
- Paid plan mix by source

Recommended definitions using current tables:

- Signup: new user profile
- Activated user: user with at least 1 row in `generations`
- Retained user: user with 2 or more generations across separate sessions or days
- Paid user: user with an active paid `billing_memberships` record or top-up activity in `credit_ledger`

Current schema already includes useful fields and tables:

- `profiles.signup_source`
- `profiles.marketing_email_opt_in`
- `generations`
- `billing_memberships`
- `credit_ledger`

Important note:

- `signup_source` exists, but the current app flow appears to use auth method values such as `password`. Repurpose or extend it to store acquisition channel values like `x`, `tiktok`, `youtube`, `product_hunt`, `creator_outreach`, and `organic_search`.

## A Simple Scorecard

Review this every Monday:

- Which post got the most saves, replies, or shares?
- Which post produced the most signups?
- Which source produced the most first generations?
- Which creators replied with real pain points?
- Which value prop got repeated back by users?

Kill channels or formats that create noise but not activated users.

## Recommended First Experiments

Run these first because they match the product.

1. Before/after series
- Format: "I fixed this thumbnail in 3 passes"
- Goal: prove capability fast

2. Creator teardown series
- Format: "3 things hurting this thumbnail"
- Goal: attract the exact user who already cares about thumbnails

3. Idea Assistant clips
- Format: topic in, 3 directions out
- Goal: show value before image generation

4. YouTube import workflow
- Format: imported old thumbnail to polished new version
- Goal: strongest upgrade story

5. Outreach-led case study
- Format: remake a small creator thumbnail and document the process
- Goal: create testimonial-ready proof

## What Success Looks Like

A good zero-dollar strategy for Thumora does not look like "huge reach."

It looks like:

- creators replying with their next video topic
- creators sending you their old thumbnail for a remake
- signups that create a thumbnail within the first day
- a few creators upgrading because the workflow saves them time every week

That is the signal to scale.

- YouTube now supports native thumbnail A/B testing for eligible long-form videos, which makes thumbnail testing a stronger marketing angle for Thumora.

- Product Hunt explicitly recommends launching a live product that users can try, and it supports self-hunting.
- Google recommends people-first content and optimized image landing pages, which fits a case-study and proof-page strategy better than thin AI-generated blog spam.
